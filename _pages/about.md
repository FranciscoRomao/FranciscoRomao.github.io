About me
======
I am a PhD student at the [Systems Research Group](https://dse.in.tum.de/) at the Technical University of Munich (TUM), under the supervision of Prof. Pramod Bhatotia. My research is centered on advancing software for Quantum Computing, with a particular focus on quantum resource management and quantum compilers.

My work spans across both Superconducting and Neutral Atoms technologies, and I am continuously exploring emerging quantum technologies to evaluate their potential applications, advantages, and limitations. Prior to my PhD, I earned a bachelor's degree in Electrical and Computer Engineering and a Master's degree in Computer Engineering (Major) and Bioinformatics (Minor) from Instituto Superior Técnico in Lisbon, where I developed a strong foundation in computing and engineering principles that continue to drive my passion for cutting-edge research in quantum computing.
